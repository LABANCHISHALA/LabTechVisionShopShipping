    Connect the real thing

    This page is the design and the workflow. A live store runs on Shopify or WordPress, with a sync app or plugin doing the supplier work.

    African payments

    • Mobile money: WordPress.org lists WooCommerce plugins for MTN MoMo, Airtel Money and Zamtel Kwacha (TuMeNy Payment, and Bila by Devdraft). Each needs its own merchant account and KYC.
    • Lipila offers mobile money, Visa/Mastercard and bank transfers in Zambia through an API; check whether it has a WooCommerce plugin when you sign up.
    • Other countries: Flutterwave, Paystack and DPO Pay each cover several African countries (M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, cards, bank). Check each one's country list and fees before you choose.
    • eBay and AliExpress: a live link needs eBay Developer keys and supplier sign-in through a sync app or plugin. The Connect buttons in Supplier sync are a preview.
    • PayPal and cards: the shop opens your PayPal.me or card payment link with the amount. Fully automatic confirmation needs the gateway plugin on the live store.
    • Bank buyers: show your Zanaco, Stanbic, FNB, Absa, Standard Chartered or Indo-Zambia account at checkout until a card gateway is live.

    WordPress + WooCommerce setup

    • Set up hosting and WordPress, install WooCommerce, then add a dropshipping plugin that imports from AliExpress and eBay.
    • Add a multi-currency plugin so shoppers can switch currency at checkout.
    • Plan for backups, SSL and updates yourself.
